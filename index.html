About

Geometry, analysis,
and the spaces between.

I am a Zelevinsky Postdoctoral Fellow at Northeastern University, working with Maxim Braverman.

I received my Ph.D. from the University of California, Santa Barbara in 2022 under the supervision of Xianzhe Dai. Before joining Northeastern, I was a Boya Postdoctoral Fellow at BICMR, Peking University, working with Xiaobo Liu. I began my studies at Nankai University.

Outside mathematics, I enjoy Yu-Gi-Oh!, cycling, and swimming.

Current projects

Current
directions

My work sits at the intersection of geometric analysis, quantum field theory, and partial differential equations—with a focus on spectral invariants, Landau–Ginzburg models, and holomorphic field theories.

01

Higher Cheeger–Müller / Bismut–Zhang theorem

Higher torsion, generalized Morse functions, and excision. Joint with Martin Puchol and Yeping Zhang.

02

Gluing formulas for global invariants

Analytic and topological gluing phenomena for spectral invariants and torsion forms.

03

Weyl laws on metric geometry

Spectral asymptotics for Schrödinger operators on noncompact spaces. Joint with Xianzhe Dai and Maxim Braverman.

04

BCOV torsion for LG models

Geometry and topology of Landau–Ginzburg B-models, in parts with Xianzhe Dai and Xinxing Tang.

05

Feynman graph integrals

Renormalization and holomorphic field theories on Kähler manifolds. Joint with Minghao Wang.
